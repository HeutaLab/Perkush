Perkush Perkush

Make a drum kit out of anything! Record a clap, a stomp or a silly noise with the camera, or grab a cartoon instrument. Then tap the pads to play your own band.

Let's play!

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap a pad

    Every coloured block is a pad. Tap an empty one to fill it with a sound.

  2. 2

    Add a sound

    Record your own: make a noise and the pad catches it on camera. Or pick a ready-made instrument.

  3. 3

    Tap to play!

    Tap the pads to hear your sounds and see your videos. Tap really fast for a drum roll.

  4. 4

    Make a band

    Mix your own sounds with instruments. Use two hands and make up a rhythm!

Meet the instruments

Tap one to hear it! You can put any of them on a pad.

Tips for great sounds

  • Get close to the thing making the sound.
  • A quiet room works best, so the pad only hears your sound.
  • Short, snappy sounds make the best drums: claps, knocks, pops and boings.
  • Want a different sound? Tap Edit, then Change or Clear.

For grown-ups

  • Private. Sounds and videos stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded or shared.
  • Camera and microphone are only used while a pad is recording, and the browser asks first.
  • Works best in Safari on iPad or iPhone, and in Chrome.
  • Keep your board. Add this page to the Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen). Safari may clear saved pads if the site isn't used for a week.
  • No sound? Turn the volume up and check that the device isn't on silent.
